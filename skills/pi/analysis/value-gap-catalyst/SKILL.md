# Value Gap Catalyst

Use `mota_finance_snapshot` before producing assessments. Treat current quote, market value, SEC facts, and recent filings as dated evidence, not timeless inputs.

## Analysis method

For each symbol:

1. Estimate a conservative fair-value range from normalized earnings, cash flow, assets, or comparable valuation context available in the tool output.
2. Measure the gap from current price and require a meaningful margin of safety before assigning a positive rating.
3. Check balance-sheet capacity, cash conversion, dilution, and liquidity. Cheapness caused by financial stress is not automatically an opportunity.
4. Identify a concrete catalyst visible in operating trends or recent filings. A valuation gap without a plausible closing mechanism deserves lower conviction.
5. Run a downside case that reflects weaker margins, slower growth, multiple compression, or refinancing risk.

Explicitly test for value traps: structurally declining revenue, deteriorating cash flow, persistent losses, weak liquidity, governance concerns, or stale/non-comparable facts.

Explain the observation date, fair-value method, margin of safety, catalyst, downside case, and conditions that invalidate the thesis. Do not use analyst target price as the sole fair-value method.
